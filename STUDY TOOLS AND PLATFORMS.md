#### 2. Critical Reflection on Tool Limitations

While the AI platform efficiently synthesized multiple policy papers and technical reports into a coherent literature summary, manual verification revealed several limitations. Quantitative statements occasionally lacked supporting evidence or were presented with overly precise statistics that could not be traced to an official publication. Some recommendations reflected generalized international logistics practices rather than the realities of agricultural production in the Davao Region. Human review was therefore essential to verify empirical claims, replace fabricated or unverifiable figures with evidence-based language, and ensure all interpretations aligned with official government reports. This audit demonstrates that AI can substantially accelerate literature synthesis, but it should function only as an assistive research tool. Final academic outputs must always undergo source validation, contextual review, and critical interpretation before being incorporated into research briefs or policy recommendations.
